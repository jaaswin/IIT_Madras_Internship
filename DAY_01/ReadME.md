# Day 01 - Introduction to IIT Madras Computer Centre

## Date

02 September 2026

## Topic

Introduction to P.G. Senapathy Centre for Computing Resources, IIT Madras

## Overview

The first day of my internship was mainly focused on understanding the **P.G. Senapathy Centre for Computing Resources (Computer Centre), IIT Madras**, its purpose, major activities, and the role it plays in supporting the institute's IT infrastructure.

The Computer Centre was established in **1973** to provide centralized computing facilities and support the academic activities of IIT Madras. Over the years, it has evolved from traditional mainframe computing facilities into a large-scale IT infrastructure supporting computing, networking, digital services, and other technology requirements of the institute.

## What the Computer Centre Does

The Computer Centre manages and supports major IT services required by the IIT Madras community. Its infrastructure provides services to:

* Faculty members
* Students
* Research scholars
* Administrative staff
* Laboratories and departments
* Hostels
* Residential areas
* Other users and services within the institute

The IT infrastructure backbone supports faculty, staff, administrative offices, laboratories, hostel zones, and residential zones across the campus.

## Major Areas of the Computer Centre

The activities of the Computer Centre are organized into five major verticals:

### 1. High Performance Computing Environment (HPCE)

HPCE provides **high-performance computing resources** for computationally intensive research and academic requirements. The Computer Centre also hosts high-performance computing facilities and supports areas such as parallel computing and batch processing.

### 2. Network

The Network team manages the **campus-wide network infrastructure**. This includes network connectivity, switches, cabling, wireless connectivity, VPN, Eduroam, and other networking services.

The network infrastructure connects different parts of the IIT Madras campus and provides connectivity to departments, laboratories, hostels, offices, and residential areas.

### 3. E-Services

E-Services provides various digital and IT services for the institute. These include:

* Email services
* Web services
* Account-related services
* Website hosting
* Storage-related services
* Other online services used by the IIT Madras community

### 4. Data Centre

The Data Centre provides the infrastructure required to keep the Computer Centre's IT systems operating reliably. It involves areas such as power management, backup power, environmental control, security, and other supporting infrastructure.

### 5. Workflow

The Workflow vertical supports various **digital processes and applications** used by the institute. These include academic, administrative, HR, student, project, finance, and other institutional workflows.

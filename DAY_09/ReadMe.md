## 5. Problem-Solving Approach

I learned that troubleshooting a network issue should be performed systematically rather than by making changes without understanding the cause.

The general approach I studied was:

```text
Identify the Problem
        ↓
Check Monitoring Data
        ↓
Identify Possible Causes
        ↓
Study the Related Protocol/Service
        ↓
Find a Possible Solution
        ↓
Test the Solution
        ↓
Observe the Result
        ↓
Compare the Results
```

This helped me understand the importance of monitoring, analysis, testing, and verification in network engineering.
